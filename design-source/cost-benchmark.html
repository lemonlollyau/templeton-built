Jordan Templeton is a fourth-generation Adelaide builder and a qualified carpenter. Templeton Built designs and builds custom homes, extensions and renovations across metropolitan Adelaide and the Hills, and prices every project open-book — the full costing schedule, including our margin.

The open book

Every line.
Including ours.

Most builders hand you a single number and ask you to trust it. We hand you the costing sheet — every trade, every allowance, every supplier quote, and our margin written plainly at the bottom.

It means you can see exactly where the money is going, question anything, and make the trade-offs yourself instead of finding out about them at handover.

About

Four generations, one city.

Templeton Built is a family building company in Thebarton. Jordan is the fourth generation of his family to build in Adelaide, and the work still carries the family name for a reason.

Jordan's great-grandfather, Peter Wyten, was raising sheds across Adelaide with his sons. That's where this starts.

The business Jordan runs today began as a property development company founded by his father. Jordan completed a carpentry apprenticeship, worked every part of the business from the tools through to project management, and gradually turned it toward what it is now: a builder of custom homes, extensions and renovations across metropolitan Adelaide and the Hills.

Why the company is deliberately small

We take on a limited number of projects each year. That isn't modesty — it's the only structure in which Jordan can genuinely be on every site. A larger operation would mean handing your build to a project manager you met once at contract signing.

It also means we're selective, and occasionally we'll tell you we're not the right builder for a project. That's a better outcome for everyone than taking work we can't give proper attention.

What we're actually good at

Old houses. Adelaide's stone villas, bungalows and character homes behave differently to new construction, and the difference between a builder who's worked on a hundred of them and one who hasn't shows up in the first fortnight.

Complicated sites. Fall, access, heritage overlays, neighbours, soil. The constraints that make architects interested tend to be the ones that make builders nervous.

Telling you the truth about money. Which is what the open book is for.

We'd rather lose a job at the quoting stage than lose your trust at the framing stage.

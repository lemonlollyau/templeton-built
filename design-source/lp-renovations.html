Common questions

Things people ask
before they commit.

Should we renovate or knock down and rebuild?

Sometimes rebuilding is the honest answer and we'll say so. It comes down to the condition of the existing structure, what your overlay allows, and how much of the original you actually want.

Can a renovation be staged?

Yes, and for many homes it should be. We plan stage one so it doesn't have to be undone later.

How do you deal with rising damp?

It's common in Adelaide's stone housing. The fix depends on the cause — ground levels, failed damp course, sealed render trapping moisture — so we diagnose before quoting a treatment.

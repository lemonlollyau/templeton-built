Custom homes

A house built around one family.

Architect-led custom homes across Adelaide and the Hills, from a bare site or a knock-down rebuild — designed, costed and built by the same team.

Start a conversationSee the work

A custom home is the only house you'll ever live in that was designed around your mornings.

We build a small number of custom homes each year, on land our clients have usually spent a long time finding. Some come to us with a completed architectural set; others come with a survey, a budget and an idea, and we bring the design team to the table.

Either way, the work starts with the site. Fall, orientation, soil classification, easements, overlays — these set more of your budget than the floor plan does, and knowing them early is what keeps a project honest.

How it runs

From first conversation
to handover.

01

Site and feasibility

We walk the land with you, pull the planning overlays, and give you a realistic cost range before you spend money on documentation. If the numbers don't work, you'll hear it here.

02

Design and documentation

Working with your architect or ours, we cost the design as it develops rather than at the end — so choices are made with the number in view, not against it.

03

The open-book contract

You receive the full costing schedule: every trade, every provisional sum, every supplier quote and our margin. Nothing is folded into a lump you can't interrogate.

04

Construction

A fixed site team, a live schedule, and a weekly walk-through with Jordan. Variations are priced and approved before work happens, never after.

05

Handover and beyond

A full defects walk, all certificates and manuals, and the statutory builder's warranty. We answer the phone after handover, which is not universal in this industry.

Common questions

Things people ask
before they commit.

Do I need an architect before contacting you?

No. Plenty of clients come to us first, and we'll introduce you to architects and designers whose work suits the house you're describing. Bringing a builder in early usually reduces design fees rather than adding to them.

Can you build from plans I already have?

Yes. We'll review the documentation, flag anything that will be expensive or difficult to build, and cost it line by line before you commit.

How long does a custom home take?

Design and approvals typically run longer than people expect, and construction depends on scale and site. We give you a stage-by-stage programme before you sign, and update it monthly.

What happens if costs move during the build?

Because the schedule is open, you can see movement as it happens rather than at the end. Provisional sums are reconciled against real quotes and shown to you.

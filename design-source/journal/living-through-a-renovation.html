On site

Living through a renovation without losing your mind

Sometimes staying put is the right call. Here's what it actually involves.

Decide honestly whether you can

The question isn't whether it's possible — it usually is. It's whether you can tolerate six to nine months of it. Households with small children, shift workers and anyone working from home should think hard.

The services are the pinch point

You can live without a formal living room for a long time. You cannot live without a functioning bathroom or a way to cook. Good staging protects those two things for as long as possible and compresses the gap when it comes.

Set the site rules up front

Start times, access routes, where trades park, whether the dog can be in the yard. Agreeing this in week one prevents most of the friction that builds over a long job.

Budget for a fortnight away

There is almost always a two-week window that is genuinely miserable. Planning to be elsewhere for it is money well spent.

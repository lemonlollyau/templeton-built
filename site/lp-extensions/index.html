Common questions

The questions worth
asking any builder.

Can we stay in the house during the work?

Often yes, depending on scope and whether kitchen or bathroom services are affected. We'll tell you honestly at feasibility stage.

How do you handle heritage overlays?

We deal with Adelaide councils regularly. Getting the front elevation and materials right early avoids a costly round of amended plans.

What if something unexpected turns up in the old structure?

We open up and investigate before pricing, and carry named allowances for what old homes hide — so discoveries change the schedule rather than blindsiding the budget.

Interior design

Plan it properly before anything comes out.

Interior design in Adelaide, led by award-winning designer Rachel Mao — with access to Templeton Built’s suppliers and pricing. Know what your renovation looks like, and what it costs, before you start.

Start your planSee the packages

Renovating costs more than it used to. Starting without a plan costs more again.

Renovation budgets rarely go wrong on the big decisions. They go wrong on the small ones made in a hurry — the tile chosen the day the tiler arrives, the vanity that doesn’t suit the plumbing, the joinery quote that lands after demolition.

Our interior design service puts those decisions first. Rachel works through the look, the selections and the drawings with you, so you start with a defined scope, real prices and a clear picture of the finished room.

You don’t need to build with us to use it. It’s made for people running their own renovation, with their own trades.

Common questions

The questions worth asking
before you renovate.

Do I have to build with Templeton?

No. The design service stands on its own. Use your own trades, or bring the plan to us later — your choice.

How much does an interior designer cost?

Our packages start from $2,000 for concept and selections, $6,000 with drawings and pricing, and $12,500 for a complete design. What you pay depends on how many rooms are involved and how far the drawings need to go.

Can you design just one room?

Yes. Concept & Selections and Plan & Price both suit a single kitchen or bathroom — a kitchen design on its own is one of the more common places to start.

Can I use my own trades?

Yes. The drawings and selections are made for your trades to quote and build from.

How long does it take?

It depends on the package and how many rooms are involved. Rachel will give you a timeframe on the first call, before you commit to anything.

What if I decide to build with Templeton later?

That happens often, and the plan is built so it can. The selections and drawings carry straight across to a build — ask us how the design fee is treated when you get there.

Is this interior decorating, or something more?

More. An interior decorator settles the look; this goes through to drawings your trades can build from and prices from our suppliers, so the look and the budget are resolved together.

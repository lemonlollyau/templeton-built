Common questions

Before you commit.

Is a knockdown rebuild cheaper than a major renovation?

Sometimes, and sometimes not. It comes down to the condition of the existing structure, what your planning overlay allows, and how much of the original you actually want to keep. We’ll give you an honest read rather than push you toward the bigger job.

Can I rebuild on my block?

In parts of Adelaide demolition isn’t permitted, or comes with tight constraints on what replaces it. That’s the first thing we check, and it takes very little time.

What happens to the existing services?

Disconnection, capping and reconnection of power, water, sewer and gas are part of the scope and appear as their own lines on your costing schedule.

How long does it take?

Approvals usually take longer than people expect, and construction depends on scale and site. You get a stage-by-stage programme before you sign, updated monthly.

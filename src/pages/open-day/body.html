Worth a close look

Details you can’t judge from a photograph.

01

The curved stone island

Stone takes a curve badly unless the substrate is built for it. Worth seeing how the join sits and what it costs to do properly.

02

Herringbone floors

A pattern that lives or dies on the setout. Stand in the doorway and look down the run.

03

Double glazing

You can hear the difference the moment the door closes — which is the part a specification sheet can’t tell you.

04

The two-way fireplace

One firebox, two rooms. Straightforward to want, less straightforward to frame and flue.

Common questions

Building in the Hills.

Why do Hills builds cost more than the same house on the plains?

Mostly site works. Excavation, retaining, driveway access, service runs and stormwater are all heavier on a sloping block, and bushfire requirements can lift the specification before design is even considered.

Do you handle bushfire attack level requirements?

Yes. BAL assessments shape material selection, glazing, decking and detailing, and we’d rather build that in early than retrofit it after documentation.

Which areas do you cover?

Across the Adelaide Hills and metropolitan Adelaide — Stirling, Aldgate, Crafers, Bridgewater, Mylor, Hahndorf and the surrounding districts.

Can you build on a steep or difficult block?

Often yes, and those are frequently the most interesting projects. The assessment happens at feasibility stage, and we’d rather tell you it isn’t viable than find out halfway through.
